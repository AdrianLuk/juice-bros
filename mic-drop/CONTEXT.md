# Mic Drop

A live karaoke vote at `/tools/mic-drop`, unlisted. The Host makes a Poll, pastes its Guest Link into the group chat, and everyone votes for their favourite Contestant from their own phone. Voting stays open all night, the Leaderboard updates live on every phone, and the Host closes the Poll at the end. Whoever leads when it closes wins.

## Language

**Poll**:
One night's vote: a list of Contestants and the Votes cast for them. It is either open or closed. A closed Poll is frozen and its Leaderboard is the result, until the Host reopens it.
_Avoid_: Event, contest, session (On Deck's word)

**Host**:
The signed-in person who creates Polls, edits their Contestants and closes them. Only someone on the Host allowlist can host.
_Avoid_: Organizer (On Deck's and Team Tally's word), admin

**Contestant**:
One entry on a Poll's list, which is whatever the Host typed. A duet is one Contestant, not two. The Host can add or remove Contestants while the Poll is open. Removing one deletes the Votes cast for them.
_Avoid_: Singer, performer, option, Player

**Guest**:
Anyone who opens the Guest Link. A Guest has no account and no name. The app knows a Guest only as one phone.
_Avoid_: Voter, user, attendee

**Vote**:
A Guest's current pick: one Contestant per phone per Poll. A Guest can change it any time while the Poll is open, and only the latest pick counts. A Guest can be left with no Vote, either before they pick or after their Contestant is removed.
_Avoid_: Ballot, rating, score

**Leaderboard**:
Every Contestant with their Vote count, highest first, shown live to everyone who has the Guest Link. When the Poll closes, the Contestant at the top is the winner. Several Contestants tied at the top are joint winners, and the app does not break the tie.
_Avoid_: Results (until the Poll is closed), standings

**Guest Link**:
The URL for one Poll that the Host shares. Opening it is the only thing a Guest needs to do to vote.
_Avoid_: Invite Link (Booking Buddy's word), share link
